

## About the Project  
**Plasma-Breaker** is a browser-based sci-fi platformer where players navigate a clandestine plasma research facility by timing jumps to shatter spinning plasma rings in a futuristic lab setting 🎮🔬 
The game’s core goal is to challenge reflexes and mastery by unlocking new test chambers as ring patterns become more intricate and environmental hazards intensify 🌪️🔥 
To date, the prototype includes two levels featuring physics-driven ring destruction mechanics, basic momentum boosts, and touch-friendly on-screen controls—laying the foundation for deeper gameplay and richer level design 🚀🛠️
